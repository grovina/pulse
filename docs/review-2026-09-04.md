# Code and mechanistics review against the PRD (2026-09-04)

Scope: the whole engine at HEAD `4c7a0a6` (iter 96, benchmark landed 2026-08-29),
graded against `docs/prd.md` — Feynman-correctness, the impose/learn hierarchy
(physics and anatomy in architecture, medical knowledge as priors, individual
variation learned), calibrated supervision (neither more nor less than the
evidence), and "the ruler is itself an approximate instrument". Four parallel
reviews (teacher, student, training signals, ruler/calibration), every finding
required to carry a measured number or an exact code path. The nine highest-impact
claims were re-verified by hand in source before this document was written.
Probe scripts live under the session scratchpad (`scratchpad/{teacher,student,
training,ruler}/`). **No code was changed.**

Severity labels: **C** = confirmed by measurement or exact code path; **P** =
plausible from reading only.

---

## 0. The picture in one paragraph

The engine has the right *skeleton* — modular neural ODE, mass action in raw
units, a learnable teacher, a persistence-skill gate — and most of the iter-91..96
structural fixes are real and measurable. What the review finds is that the
**same three diseases recur across all four layers**, and that iter 96's own
headline numbers were partly measured through them:

1. **Frame bugs** (normalized-vs-raw, window-offset-vs-absolute, 0/1 conventions)
   — this is at least the *fifth* instance of the family (iter-90 Sg, iter-95
   consumption, and now the stress cascade, benchmark calibration windows, and
   the rules' sleep arm). Each one silently makes a whole channel inert or
   inverted.
2. **Conservation is declared a layer-1 law but is not enforced anywhere carbon
   moves**: the teacher books hepatic glucose output three times, the student's
   glycogen is a shadow pool, the gut kernel delivers 3.5x the ingested mass and
   ends with a hard step, the enterohepatic loop refills from nothing.
3. **Supervision is mis-sized in both PRD directions at once**: dense pointwise
   fitting of a teacher we know is wrong (band below noise, 96 steps/epoch) while
   the entire literature stack gets 25 optimizer steps in the whole run, cohort
   targets are scored per individual against individual sigma, the coupling
   prior ignores its own magnitude ranges, and the teacher violates 22 of the 61
   rules the student is asked to satisfy.

And the **ruler** measures through a calibration objective that is a different
forward map from the scorer, with no prior, no validation, no fingerprint of the
teacher that generated 10 of its 48 episodes.
